## Course description

This course will provide you with a foundational introduction to artificial intelligence (AI)
in the context of the management in organizations. By "foundational", we will mean a set of
ideas that have been, and which remain, most important to the AI field since it formally
emerged around 80 years ago.

By the end of our course, you will have learned diverse AI ideas that, even as AI techniques
change, will be relevant throughout your career. You will also have learned to think critically
about AI, reflecting on how AI ideas make diverse assumptions, and even belong to different
paradigms. As we compare and contrast assumptions and paradigms about AI, you will feel
empowered to begin to develop your own theories for thinking about and practically managing
and organizing AI systems.

The ultimate goals of this course are that you are able to develop your own theories about AI
in ways that (1) faithfully reflect phenomena in the world that you care about, (2) are grounded
in foundational AI ideas, beyond current fashions, and (3) are actionable as AI systems that you
can actually think about, build or participate in. A final goal of the course is also for you to
see how intrinsically fun it is to study AI ideas for their own sake.
