Every concept you are working with. Add one below; hover a row for ✎ rename · ⇄ recategorize · ✕ delete. Drag a concept up or down to reorder it, or drag it to the board to place it there. Green rows are already on the board. A node on the board is its concept: rename one and the other follows, and anything you sketch with + Concept appears here too.

    Drag a concept onto a type or subtype heading to move it there, or drag it above or below another concept to reorder it. Concept types can also be edited: click the coloured dot to change a colour, click the name to rename, ⊞ makes a subtype, + adds a concept, ✕ removes a type. A node on the board takes the colour of its type.
